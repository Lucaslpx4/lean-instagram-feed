Privacy Policy

LEAN Instagram Feed · last updated 11 November 2026

This application is operated by Lucas Leparoux for Lean B.V. Its only purpose is to display the public Instagram posts of the LEAN Instagram professional account on the website leanchicken.com.

What the application accesses

With the explicit authorisation of the account holder, and through the official Instagram API, the application reads:

  • the username of the connected Instagram professional account
  • the public media published by that account: image URL, caption, permalink and publication date

What the application does not do

The application does not read direct messages, comments, followers, audience insights or any personal data belonging to the people who follow the account. It never publishes, edits or deletes anything on the Instagram account. It does not collect any data from visitors of leanchicken.com.

Storage

Images and captions retrieved from the account are copied into the Shopify file storage of leanchicken.com so they can be shown on the website. Nothing is shared with any third party, and nothing is used for advertising or profiling.

Withdrawing access

The account holder can withdraw access at any time from Instagram, under Settings, then Apps and websites. Access stops immediately and no further data is read.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any stored data, write to the contact address below. Stored images and captions are removed within 30 days.
